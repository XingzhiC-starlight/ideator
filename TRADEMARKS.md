Ideator's icon, which identify the app that Xingzhi Cheng publishes.

- say that your work is based on Ideator, or works with it, in plain words such as "a fork of
  Ideator";
- keep the name and icon in a copy you build only for yourself.

- give a copy you distribute, changed or not, the name "Ideator", a name that could be mistaken for
  it, or Ideator's icon;

If you distribute a fork, give it its own name, icon and bundle identifier.
